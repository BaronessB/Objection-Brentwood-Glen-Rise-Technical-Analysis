A Narrative of Siloed Failure: Objection to Brentwood, Glen Rise

This formal objection refutes Planning Applications 26/00322/VAC, 24/01264/OUT, and 25/01757/REM. The proposal relies on a technical narrative that fails to account for how physical site constraints—specifically a 5-6m vertical fall, cohesive clay soils, and unverified infilled ground—intersect to create substantial risk.
